# English localization pilot

Ten samples from the [Chinese candidate](Chinese_Localization_Draft.md), covering eight institutions and two action tooltips. Institution names and ability labels are proposed English names. Each institution opens with one short introduction, followed by the effects and conditions.

## Scholarly Circle · 学者结社

`LOC_SPC_RESEARCH_INSTITUTION_1_HELP`

Scholars gather here to study and lay the groundwork for the city's research.

**Basic Specialist Support**

Each working Research specialist provides +3 `[ICON_Food]` Food and +3 `[ICON_Production]` Production.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

学者在此结社研习，为城市的科研事业奠定基础。

**基础专家支持**

每名在岗科研专家额外提供 +3 `[ICON_Food]` 食物和 +3 `[ICON_Production]` 生产力。

</details>

## Patrons’ Guild · 赞助人行会

`LOC_SPC_CULTURE_INSTITUTION_2_HELP`

Steady patronage and commissions help literature, art, and music flourish.

**Arts Patronage**

- This city's Theater Square provides +1 `[ICON_Housing]` Housing, plus 1 for each tier of eligible buildings it contains. Each tier counts once.
- Each working Culture specialist provides +2 base `[ICON_GreatWriter]` Great Writer points, +2 base `[ICON_GreatArtist]` Great Artist points, and +2 base `[ICON_GreatMusician]` Great Musician points.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

稳定的资助与委托，让文学、艺术与音乐在城中长久兴盛。

**文艺赞助**

- 本城剧院广场 +1 `[ICON_Housing]` 住房。每个已有合格普通建筑的等级再 +1，同级只计一次。
- 每名在岗文化专家额外提供 +2 `[ICON_GreatWriter]` 大作家、+2 `[ICON_GreatArtist]` 大艺术家、+2 `[ICON_GreatMusician]` 大音乐家基础点数。

</details>

## Academic Directorate · 学术总署

`LOC_SPC_RESEARCH_INSTITUTION_4_HELP`

Well-equipped campuses support deeper inquiry and a lasting tradition of scholarship.

**Research Infrastructure**

- Each working Research specialist provides +1 base `[ICON_Science]` Science per point of infrastructure depth in this city's Campus, up to +10 per specialist.

**Academic Stewardship**

- Each working Research specialist adds +1 base `[ICON_Science]` Science to each eligible, unpillaged tier 1–4 Campus building in this city.

**Academic Tradition**

- This city starts building Academic Tradition when its Research Potential reaches Level 4. It continues as long as the city specializes in Research, even while Level 4 is inactive. Progress pauses if the city leaves Research and resumes if it returns.
- With Research active at Level 4, this city gains +5% `[ICON_Science]` Science, plus 5 percentage points for every 10 accumulated turns at Standard Speed, up to +25%.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

完备的学院设施让学者得以深入研究，学术传统也随岁月积累。

**科研基础设施**

- 本城学院每 1 点基础设施深度，使每名在岗科研专家额外提供 +1 基础 `[ICON_Science]` 科技值，最高 +10。

**学术主持**

- 每名在岗科研专家使本城每座完好的普通学院一至四级建筑 +1 基础 `[ICON_Science]` 科技值。

**学术传统**

- 科研潜力达到四级后开始积累。四级启用时，本城初始 +5% `[ICON_Science]` 科技值。
- 保有科研身份时持续积累，即使暂未启用四级。转出科研后暂停，转回后继续积累。标准速度下，每累计 10 回合再提高 5 个百分点，最高 +25%。

</details>

## Humanities Federation · 人文联合会

`LOC_SPC_CULTURE_INSTITUTION_3_HELP`

Great Works from many eras find new interpretations and enrich the life of the city.

**Dialogue of Eras**

- This project takes 1 full turn of Production. Each city can complete it once per game era. A completion counts toward the era in which the project began.
- On completion, each distinct era represented in the city's eligible collection adds 5 percentage points to its `[ICON_Tourism]` Tourism bonus for Great Works. The city keeps the accumulated bonus. It applies while Culture is active at Level 3 or higher.

**Cultivated Taste**

- Each eligible, unpillaged building gains +1 `[ICON_Tourism]` Tourism per era represented by the city's current eligible Great Works.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

不同时代的巨作在此汇聚，旧作有了新的诠释，艺术也渐渐融入城市生活。

**时代对话**

- 项目需 1 个完整生产回合。每城每个游戏时代限完成一次，按开工时的时代计。
- 完成时，合格馆藏每涵盖一个作品时代，巨作本身的 `[ICON_Tourism]` 旅游业绩倍率永久累积 5 个百分点。文化启用等级达到三级时生效。

**风雅熏陶**

- 本城合格馆藏每涵盖一个作品时代，每座完好的合格普通建筑 +1 `[ICON_Tourism]` 旅游业绩。

</details>

## Academy of the Liberal Arts · 博雅总院

`LOC_SPC_CULTURE_INSTITUTION_4_HELP`

Scholars draw on many fields to reinterpret Great Works and find inspiration in the art and customs of other civilizations.

**Extended Meaning**

- Eligible Great Works gain extra yields based on the infrastructure depth of this city's qualifying district types. Theming bonuses apply. Dialogue of Eras boosts only the Great Works' own Tourism. Government Plazas and Diplomatic Quarters currently grant no extra Culture through this ability.

**Great Works Inspiration**

- This city gains +3% `[ICON_GreatPerson]` Great Person points for each era represented in its eligible collection, up to +24%. Only types with base points in this city benefit.

**Humanities Expedition**

- Train a Humanities Expedition and send it to a foreign major civilization you have met. Your civilization can have 1 at a time. Each source city earns 1 Cultural Insight the first time each mission type succeeds in a given civilization.
- Eligible Cultural Insights increase the source city's `[ICON_Tourism]` Tourism. If that city collects all three types from a single civilization, Trade Routes can grant `[ICON_Culture]` Culture to eligible working specialists in connected cities.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

学者借各领域的见解重读巨作，也把目光投向异国的风土与艺术。

**意义延展**

- 每件合格巨作从本城符合条件的专业领域获得额外产出。基础设施深度决定收益。可受主题化加成影响，不受「时代对话」放大。目前不计市政广场与外交区的文化追加。

**巨作启迪**

- 本城合格馆藏每涵盖一个作品时代，已有基础产出的 `[ICON_GreatPerson]` 伟人点数 +3%，最高 +24%。

**人文考察团**

- 可训练人文考察团，全国同时最多 1 支。派往已遇见的外国主要文明。每个来源城市对每个目标文明的每类任务首次成功时，取得 1 份文化见闻。
- 有效见闻提高来源城市的 `[ICON_Tourism]` 旅游业绩，同一来源城市集齐同一文明的三类见闻后，可通过商路为合格的在岗专家提供 `[ICON_Culture]` 文化值。

</details>

## Engineering Bureau · 工程局

`LOC_SPC_INDUSTRY_INSTITUTION_3_HELP`

Engineers turn construction experience into standards and organize crews for new projects.

**Standardization**

- +10% `[ICON_Production]` Production toward districts and buildings matching a template. Templates can be shared through Trade Routes. Only the highest bonus applies to a given target.

**Construction Crews**

- Unlocks the tier 1–3 Construction Crew projects. While Industry is active at Level 3, each crew costs 150% of its base Production contribution to form. Each source city can support up to 2 crews at a time.
- Consume a crew to contribute `[ICON_Production]` Production to the Wonder the city is currently building. Excess Production does not carry over.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

工程师将建造经验编成规范，并组织施工队支援新的建设。

**标准化**

- 建造符合模板的区域和建筑时，+10% `[ICON_Production]` 生产力。模板可沿商路共享，同一目标只取最高加成。

**施工队**

- 解锁一级至三级施工队组建项目，工业启用三级时，组建成本为基础施工力的 150%。每个来源城市同时最多支持 2 支。
- 消耗施工队，为当前在建奇观投入 `[ICON_Production]` 生产力。超出所需的部分不结转。

</details>

## Directorate of Civil Engineering · 土木工程总局

`LOC_SPC_INDUSTRY_INSTITUTION_4_HELP`

Engineers study the Wonders of the past to inform the great works of today.

**Engineering Tradition**

- This city can complete 1 study for each distinct era of Wonders your civilization has built. Count each Wonder's own era. Each study takes 1 full turn of Production.
- While Industry is active at Level 4, each completed study raises the Standardization `[ICON_Production]` Production bonus and the purchase discount for eligible buildings by 2 percentage points each.

**Engineering Practice**

- Unlocks the tier 4 and 5 Construction Crew projects. At 1, 3, and 6 Wonders built in this city, crew costs fall to 140%, 130%, and 120% of their base Production contribution, respectively.

**Macro-Engineering**

- +10%, +20%, or +30% `[ICON_Production]` Production toward Wonders from 1, 2, or at least 3 eras before your civilization’s current era, respectively. This bonus also applies to Construction Crew contributions.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

工程师研习历代奇观的建造经验，把过去的巧思用到今天的大工程上。

**工程传统**

- 按本文明亲建奇观的所属时代计算。每涵盖一个时代，本城可研习一次。每次占用 1 个完整生产回合，完成后积累 1 层。
- 四级启用时，每层成果使「标准化」的 `[ICON_Production]` 建造加成和合格建筑的购买折扣各提高 2 个百分点。

**工程实践**

- 解锁四级、五级施工队组建项目。本城累计建成 1、3、6 座奇观后，组建成本依次降至基础施工力的 140%、130%、120%。

**巨构工程学**

- 建造比本文明当前时代早 1、2 或至少 3 个时代的奇观时，分别获得 +10%、+20% 或 +30% `[ICON_Production]` 生产力。施工队的投入也享受此加成。

</details>

## Capital Trust · 资本信托

`LOC_SPC_COMMERCE_INSTITUTION_4_HELP`

Investors fund new projects along trade routes, give old industries a fresh start, and build a reputation over time.

**Development Investment**

- Invest `[ICON_Gold]` Gold in a city reached by a direct domestic Trade Route from this city. Eligible buildings in the target district receive +50% `[ICON_Production]` Production. The contract ends when the next eligible building is completed.

**Asset Restructuring**

- An Asset Restructuring Team can liquidate assets in one of your specialized cities with Potential at Level 2 or higher. This permanently lowers that city's Potential by 1 level. During restructuring, non-Food yields and `[ICON_Food]` surplus Food used for growth are reduced by 75%.
- After 5 full turns, use the team to allocate assets in another eligible district in the same city. This consumes the team and changes the city's specialization.

**Commercial Reputation**

- Once the Level 4 institution is established, the city earns 1 Reputation per full turn while it specializes in Commerce, up to 40 at Standard Speed. It loses its Reputation if it leaves Commerce.
- While Commerce is active at Level 4, each point of Reputation increases Gold from Commercialization by 0.5% and the share of principal returned after a failed Venture Investment by 0.5 percentage points. At the cap, income is 20% higher and failed Venture Investments return 70% of principal.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

资金沿商路投向新的建设，旧产业有了新的出路，信誉也在经营中逐渐积累。

**发展投资**

- 向本城发出的直接国内商路所连接的城市投资 `[ICON_Gold]` 金币。目标区域建造合格建筑时，+50% `[ICON_Production]` 生产力。下一座合格建筑完成后，合同结束。

**资产重组**

- 重组团队可在潜力至少二级的己方专业城市清算资产，永久扣除 1 级潜力。重组期间，非食物产出和用于人口增长的 `[ICON_Food]` 余粮减少 75%。
- 计满 5 个完整回合后，须在同城另一合格专业区域手动配置资产，消耗团队并转换专业。

**商业信誉**

- 建立四级机构后，保有商业身份时每完整回合积累 1 点信誉，上限 40（标准速度）。转出商业后信誉清零。
- 四级启用时，每点信誉使商业化收益 +0.5%，风险投资失败的本金返还比例提高 0.5 个百分点。最高分别为 +20% 和返还 70%。

</details>

## Development Investment Contract · 发展投资合同

`LOC_SPC_DEVELOPMENT_TERMS`

+50% `[ICON_Production]` Production toward eligible buildings in the target district. This bonus adds to Standardization.

The contract ends when the next eligible building in the district is finished, whether built, bought with `[ICON_Gold]` Gold, or completed another way. It can be a different building from the one in the quote.

The contract stays in place if the city switches production.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

目标区域的合格建筑获得 +50% `[ICON_Production]` 建造生产力，与「标准化」加成相加。

该区域下一座合格建筑完成时合同结束，包括金币购买或其他方式直接完成。所建建筑可以与报价时不同。

切换建造目标不会取消合同。

</details>

`LOC_SPC_CREW_BASE_CONFIRM`

Consume this Construction Crew to contribute `{1_Input}` base `[ICON_Production]` Production to the Wonder the city is currently building. Applicable bonuses can increase the contribution.

This cannot be undone.

<details>
<summary>中文对照</summary>

消耗此施工队，为当前在建奇观投入 `{1_Input}` 点基础 `[ICON_Production]` 生产力。适用的建造加成可提高实际投入。

确认后无法撤销。

</details>

## Review notes

- **Levels and tiers.** Active and Potential specialization levels use “Level.” Building and Construction Crew tiers use “tier.”
- **Rates.** Percentage points describe additions to the Great Works Tourism bonus, Standardization bonuses, and the refund rate. Percentages describe yield bonuses and the final share of principal returned.
- **City scope.** Accrued Academic Tradition and completed Dialogue of Eras bonuses are recorded for the city. Cultural Insights are earned by the expedition's source city. The English wording preserves those distinctions.

The current [institution tooltip builder](../../../Mod/InstitutionPresentation.lua#L107) looks for the fullwidth colon after a localized heading. Before integrating the sample, the six English headings with live statistics need matching English metric labels and a recognized English separator.
