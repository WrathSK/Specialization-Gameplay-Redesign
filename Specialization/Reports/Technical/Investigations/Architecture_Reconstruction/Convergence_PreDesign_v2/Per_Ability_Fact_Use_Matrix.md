## Reading key and global fences

This matrix has **60 distinct ability/input/use rows**. It is not a fact-type permission list. **S** = successful current strict read; **R** = retained input used to recompute; **F** = temporary same-reference preservation of actual applied output plus explicit error, not silent indefinite HELD; **A history** = protected saved authority, not a cache; **V** = explicitly qualified UI projection. **N** = independently confirmed owner/reference/identity/REALLOCATING or ability-qualification exit takes priority at its actual scope.

Decision status: **A** existing/owner-approved constraint; **P** specifically permitted direction but lifetime/recovery/use details open; **C** Codex candidate, not approved; **OPEN** needs clarification. A service retaining a value does not approve any C row. S failure never implies0, empty collection, canceled project or confirmed exit. On first no-value, do not fabricate an input or installed output. Cold raw facts reconstruct; no generic persistence.

Every retained candidate must declare last-confirmed witness, raw vs derived value, owner/token/reference lifetime, confirmed-negative precedence, availability metadata, meaningful recovery wake and first-read behavior before v3 acceptance. ## Matrix-specific conclusions

1. **Cross clarification:** current ResearchCross has no D dependency. Its adjacency sample and current district qualification are different cells. H2's example must not introduce D as a new gameplay input.
2. **Numeric depth is not a building set:** housing/Chair/Aesthetic qualification can depend on real tier/presence/completion/pillage. Approving a D number cannot retain a building already confirmed absent.
3. **Worker sampling is not worker policy:** current shared getter/phase reuse does not approve every ability's retained-worker behavior or settle native/HD auto-assignment semantics.
4. **D2 precedence:** Commercialization's strict preference does not silently revoke approved reference-valid source contribution retention after confirmed topology. It also does not authorize Capital to use that passive state.
5. **Reputation cross-use:** F47 and F49 intentionally differ. Passive0 bonus is permitted; a new venture refund must not lock an unconfirmed effective age as0. F50 is the mode-specific remaining choice.
6. **UI cannot resolve business staleness:** actual applied, desired preview, retained/pending and current qualification are distinct. D11 is approved, but connecting its publication is implementation work.

R1 reviews candidate cells in named groups; R6 handles Capital mode readiness. The other rows retain approved constraints or accurately scoped evidence gaps. No reader, fact service or policy was changed in this task.
