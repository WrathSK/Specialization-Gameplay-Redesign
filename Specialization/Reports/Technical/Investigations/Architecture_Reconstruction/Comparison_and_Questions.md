## Cross-cutting architectural concerns to review

These are evidence-led review points, not redesign proposals or a defect count.

- **Two-way logical dependency:** Store imports profession models, while ability owners consume Store APIs. That is a real family-level coupling. It does not establish a recursive include cycle or prove these policies should move elsewhere. [Store imports](../../../../../Mod/CityProgressionStore.lua#L4), [owner composition dependencies](../../../../../Mod/Gameplay.lua#L972).
- **Shared mutable composition surface:** ExposedMembers.SPC_P0 exposes services, callbacks, projections and diagnostics, not one immutable DTO. Startup dependencies, FactBindings declarations and owner-local guards repeat names for different purposes. Review duties and availability contracts before judging the overlap. [root construction](../../../../../Mod/Gameplay.lua#L10), [binding lookup](../../../../../Mod/FactConsumerBindings.lua#L6).
- **Multiple update entrances:** Native district events can reach Store, D and network paths; a subsequent committed identity event can reach network again. The once-per-owner guard is local to one fact flush, not global deduplication across all listeners. [constructed](../../../../../Mod/CityProgressionStore.lua#L2102), [D hooks](../../../../../Mod/DistrictCompleteness.lua#L265), [binding phase0](../../../../../Mod/FactConsumerBindings.lua#L11), S27–S29.
- **Different meanings of “current”:** native balance, Store history, ACTIVE, network proof, retained contribution, desired effect, applied carrier and UI snapshot have different validity windows. This is important when planning future abilities; no one displayed level proves them all current. See F01–F13.
- **Shared mechanism versus independent policy:** CarrierSet, timed readers and shared anchor/worker facts do not merge eligibility, pillage, cancellation, settlement or historical ownership rules. Existing differences cannot automatically be classified as duplication to remove. See T04/T19/T20 and E06–E08.
