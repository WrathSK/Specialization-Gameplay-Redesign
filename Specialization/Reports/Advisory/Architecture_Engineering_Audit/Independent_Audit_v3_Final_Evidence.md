## 2. Method

- Falsification, not endorsement. Each assertion below asks for a concrete reachable counterexample against (a) the candidate's claims about current code and (b) the candidate's target rules.
- Sub-auditors: one fresh read-only agent per group, at most two running at once, each writing its notes incrementally to its own scratch file. The Advisor verifies every REFUTED/GAP claim and every High/Critical item against the cited lines before it becomes a finding.
- Verdicts: **CONFIRMED** (claim holds; lines cited), **REFUTED** (counterexample with file:line and scenario), **GAP** (target rule incomplete or contradictory, so implementation would need an unmade decision or would likely be reworked), **UNVERIFIED** (needs a runtime/native fact; minimal verification stated).
- Severity: **Critical** = plan as written yields wrong irreversible outcomes (money, units, history) or cannot be implemented without changing approved gameplay. **High** = structural gap/contradiction likely to force rework of an O/PU package or produce incorrect player-visible behaviour, or a current defect the plan misclassifies. **Medium** = incompleteness or a missing gate that would be caught later at moderate cost. **Low** = clarity or minor evidence mismatch.
- Evidence labels keep their repository meaning: STATIC_CONFIRMED, LOCAL_SIMULATION_PASS (existing records only, not rerun), USER_GAME_TEST_PASS/FAIL (named scenarios only), UNVERIFIED. Nothing was executed in this audit.
